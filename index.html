Questions people ask first

What if I barely use it one month?

That's normal and completely fine — some weeks are dense, some are quiet. You're paying for access and continuity across a season, not a session quota. And the week-6 check-in exists precisely so a busy stretch doesn't quietly turn into "I wasted it."

Can I really message anytime?

Yes — send a voice note whenever something snags. I reply within 48 hours on working days. Those clear edges are what let me be genuinely dependable rather than vaguely always-on.

Is this coaching or consulting?

Neither label matters much. In practice it's a thinking partner: I don't tell you what to do or run your project. I help you clear the internal friction so your own decisions come faster and cleaner.

What if I'm not sure I've "jumped" yet?

Then Sailing may be early for you. If you're still weighing whether to make a change, Compass or The Crossing will serve you better — and I'll tell you honestly on the Discovery Call.

One last thing

The bravest people I know still get stuck. They just don't stay stuck as long, because they stopped trying to do it all alone. Keeping a thinking partner in the boat isn't a sign you're struggling. Plenty of people who are building keep one close for exactly this reason.
